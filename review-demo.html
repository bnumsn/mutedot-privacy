Google Play Policy Walkthrough — MuteDot (app.mutedot.wear)

Screen-captured demonstration on Wear OS 5 (Round 480×480) covering both the AccessibilityService (TYPE_ACCESSIBILITY_OVERLAY) Prominent Disclosure and the Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE) hardware microphone mute lock.

Walkthrough Sequence Shown in Video

  1. Launch MuteDot on Wear OS (MIC OPEN) and toggle hardware mute lock (MIC LOCKED, engaging MicGuardService ongoing notification before Accessibility is enabled).
  2. Tap Enable Edge Status Dot › to present the full in-app Accessibility Overlay Prominent Disclosure (AccessibilityDisclosureActivity).
  3. Scroll through the disclosure, tap Agree & Open Settings, and enable MuteDot Status Dot in Wear OS Accessibility Settings.
  4. Return to MuteDot and the Wear OS Watch Face, demonstrating the non-interactive Green Dot (MIC OPEN) and Red Dot (MIC LOCKED) updating live on the watch bezel.
